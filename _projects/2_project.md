From January 2024 to July 2024, I worked on a project focused on detecting cracks and fractures for inspection at the campus of the Federal University of Minas Gerais, using an unmanned aerial vehicle at the Flight Simulation Laboratory.

During these months, I mainly focused on building a simulated environment in Blender that resembled the university campus for testing purposes through simulation. In addition, I integrated the created environment into the ROS system. I also implemented basic code for crack detection using computer vision.

This first contact with the scientific environment made me understand the need for systematic, high-quality testing and how essential a good simulation environment is for that purpose.
